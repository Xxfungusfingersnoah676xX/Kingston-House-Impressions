Welcome to Kingston House Impressions

A Destination

Kingston House Impressions is located inside a beautifully restored Queenslander built in the early 1900s. The restaurant blends historic charm with modern dining, offering a warm and elegant atmosphere for all occasions.

Our History

The Kingston House property has served many roles over the past century — from a private residence to a doctor’s surgery, maternity hospital, tea rooms, and now a refined dining venue. Today, it stands as one of Gympie’s most iconic heritage restaurants, celebrating local produce, fine wine, and exceptional service.
